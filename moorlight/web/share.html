        Controls

        Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom (zoom only magnifies; it never moves you into the structure), move two fingers to pan. The bar at the right edge of the view is the structure from front to back. Its window is the layer you see: drag it to look deeper or less deep, drag its ends to make the layer thinner or thicker, tap the bar to jump. The molecule keeps turning about the same point (the white dot on the bar); double-tap an atom to make it the rotation centre. Tap an atom for its label and the map values there; double-tap to centre on it. Residues in front of the one at the centre that cover it are hidden, with their density; residues bonded to it stay. Hide in front under Structure (or C) turns this on and off. Sliders set each map's contour level. On a trackpad, two-finger scrolling changes the level of the first visible map, two-finger click and drag pans, pinching zooms. With a mouse, the wheel changes the level, right-drag or Shift-drag pans and Ctrl+wheel zooms. F shows the view alone, , . move in depth and [ ] change the slab, A hides the structure, L fixes the slice, S saves it. The slice faces you and turns with the molecule until you fix it; tilt it with the two angle sliders, or label three or more atoms and put it through them. A thick slice shows the strongest value within the thickness, so features just off the plane appear as well. Live plane off hides the moving plane and leaves the saved ones. Save plane keeps a plane in place (with its own colour) so you can place another. Planes show only in Slice mode, with the map in use; with no map shown they are empty. In the plane list, tap a name to look at that plane; Copy adds a parallel plane at the copy offset; Edit makes it the live plane again. Planes marked "(yours)" are kept in this browser; the others come with the page.
